Numbers, forum

Same readings as the ebook check. Capitals slip and the grammar is a little off. The number itself is still the number. The heading is what you should hear.

A comma groups thousands. A missing comma is still thousands. The word million is million.

the city counted 1,204 votes and there is still 12000 people who hasnt been counted.

the debt is 3,200,000 which is real bad, and the fund holds 3.2 million shares anyway.

there was 1984 seats and half of them was empty.

One is singular, even in a sloppy line.

the hall held 1 guest. then it held 2 guests and the fire marshal was mad.

A decimal point stays with the number. It does not end the sentence.

pi is about 3.14 and i still cant remember the rest.

the rate fell to 0.05, the score is 2.0, the index sits at 0.5. none of that is good.

An ordinal is the word. 11th, 12th, and 13th are eleventh, twelfth, and thirteenth.

she finished 1st, then he finished 22nd, and someone got 11th.

the 21st amendment passed, she finished 3rd, he finished 13th. what a night.

A fraction is spoken as a fraction.

add 1/2 cup, or 3/4 if your feeling brave, about 2/3 of the class agreed anyway.

the gap is 1 1/2 inches and the song is in 3/4 time so good luck.

A percent sign is percent.

the tax is 50% which is insane, inflation was 3.5%, and a 100% score is rare.

the change was 0.5% and people still complained.

A dash between numbers is to.

read pages 12-14 if you want, the final score was 3-2, kids aged 5-12 was everywhere.

A leading minus is negative.

the change is -3. it was -5 degrees overnight and the revision is -3.2 points, not sure how.

Zero is zero.

the count is 0 and the score is 0-0 and im bored.

A phone number or an extension is digits.

call 415-555-0199 or the desk line is (212) 555-0148, dial +1 415 555 0199 if your abroad.

call extension 4412 and ask for the form.

A highway or a flight is grouped. A room number is a cardinal. Leading zeros stay digit by digit.

take highway 101, flight 880 is late again, the room is 12, the code is 007 dont lose it.

A chapter or a page number is a cardinal.

chapter 4 is short and the note is on page 12, scene 3 is the quiet one i think.

A primed height is feet and inches.

hes 5'7" and the door is 6'2" so he is fine.

A roman numeral in a name or a title is the number, even in lowercase.

henry viii ruled england, elizabeth ii opened the term, world war ii ended in 1945.

chapter iv is short, volume ii is shorter, super bowl lviii was in las vegas.

type ii diabetes is common and louis xiv was king, apparently.

A roman list is spoken as numbers.

ok the rules are (i) pay rent (ii) keep the peace (iii) insure (iv) vacate. dont argue.

as follows: i) rent; ii) noise; iii) pets. thats the whole post.

One lowercase marker is spoken as a number.

(iv) vacate on notice, they was serious about that one.

One capital marker stays as written. Two together are spoken as numbers.

the chart says morphine (IV) fluids, dont mix that up.

see (IV) and (XII), both of them, the note is a mess.

A period ends the sentence first, so the marker stays as letters.

we finished the main text. vi. Appendix follows, if you even get that far.
