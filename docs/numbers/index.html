Numbers

Play each group in Listen2Page. The heading is what you should hear. Years and clock times are in Dates. Money is in Currencies. Measures are in Units.

A comma groups thousands. The word million is million.

The town has 3 shops.

The city counted 1,204 votes.

Attendance was 12,000.

The debt is 3,200,000.

The fund holds 3.2 million shares.

There were 1,984 seats.

One is singular.

The hall held 1 guest.

The hall held 2 guests.

A decimal point stays with the number.

Pi is about 3.14.

The rate fell to 0.05.

The score is 2.0.

The index sits at 0.5.

An ordinal is the word, not the digits plus th. 11th, 12th, and 13th are eleventh, twelfth, and thirteenth.

She finished 1st.

He finished 2nd.

She finished 3rd.

He finished 4th.

The 21st amendment passed.

He finished 22nd.

She finished 23rd.

She finished 11th.

He finished 12th.

She finished 13th.

A fraction is spoken as a fraction. A mixed number keeps the whole and the fraction.

Add 1/2 cup.

Use 3/4 of the sheet.

About 2/3 of the class agreed.

The gap is 1 1/2 inches.

The song is in 3/4 time.

A percent sign is percent.

The tax is 50%.

Inflation was 3.5%.

A 100% score is rare.

The change was 0.5%.

A dash between numbers is to.

Read pages 12-14.

The final score was 3-2.

Children aged 5-12 may enter.

A leading minus is negative.

The change is -3.

It was -5 degrees overnight.

The revision is -3.2 points.

Zero is zero.

The count is 0.

The score is 0-0.

A phone number or an extension is digits, not one cardinal.

Call 415-555-0199.

The desk line is (212) 555-0148.

Dial +1 415 555 0199.

Call extension 4412.

A highway or a flight is grouped. A short room number is a cardinal. A code with leading zeros is digit by digit.

Take Highway 101.

Flight 880 is on time.

The room is 12.

The code is 007.

A chapter or a page number is a cardinal.

Chapter 4 is short.

The note is on page 12.

Scene 3 is the quiet one.

A primed height is feet and inches.

He stands 5'7" tall.

The door is 6'2".

A roman numeral in a name or a title is the number.

Henry VIII ruled England.

Elizabeth II opened the term.

Louis XIV was king.

World War II ended in 1945.

Chapter IV is short.

Volume II is shorter.

Super Bowl LVIII was in Las Vegas.

Type II diabetes is common.

A roman list is spoken as numbers.

(i) pay rent; (ii) keep the peace; (iii) insure; (iv) vacate.

as follows: i) rent; ii) noise; iii) pets.

One lowercase marker is spoken as a number.

(iv) vacate on notice.

One capital marker stays as written. Two together are spoken as numbers.

morphine (IV) fluids.

see (IV) and (XII).

A period ends the sentence first, so the marker stays as letters.

vi. Appendix follows.
