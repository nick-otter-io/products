Dates, forum

A full date is month, day, and year, even when the commas are gone.

the meeting is january 5 2024 i think, she was born march 3 1991.

monday march 3 2024 was a holiday and nobody told me.

Day, then month, then year, is still that date.

the letter is dated 5 january 2024, the treaty was signed on 14 july 1789. dont quote me.

A year is spoken as a year.

she was born in 1984 which is wild, the act passed in 1776, the flight is in 2024.

A month abbreviation is the month. aug is August.

we leave in jan and the note is dated jan 5, sept 12 is a friday if the calendar is right.

the term starts in sep. the fair opens aug 8. we leave in august and its already hot.

A numeric date is month, then day. 10/13 is October 13.

the date is 10/13 dont be late, or 10/13/2024 if you need the year.

the form says 04/05/2024 and the stamp says 2024-10-13, same month different day.

A day of the month is an ordinal.

the shop is closed on the 1st, file it by the 2nd, the hearing is on the 3rd.

the 22nd is a friday and we met on the 1st of may, i think.

A weekday abbreviation is the weekday.

the class meets mon and wed, the office is closed sat, see you on fri maybe.

A range is from one to the other. A decade is the decade. A missing apostrophe does not change the '90s.

the conference runs march 3-5 and the years 1999-2001 was quiet.

she grew up in the 1990s, the song is pure 90s, the 21st century is young i guess.

An era marker stays with the year.

he died in 44 bc, the wall dates to 122 ad, the site is from 300 bce and the chapel is from 1100 ce.

A quarter is the quarter of that year.

the report covers q3 2024 and the fiscal year is fy2025, numbers is late as usual.

A clock time is the time of day. 3pm is three p.m. A missing space does not change it.

meet me at 9 am or 9 a.m. whatever, call me at 3pm dont be late.

the train leaves at 3:05 pm, the alarm is 7:05 a.m., lunch is at 12:00.

the shift ends at 18:30 and office hours are 9-5, noon is 12:00 pm, midnight is 12:00 am.
