Same readings as the ebook check. The unit token stays. The sentence around it does not. A slash is still per. One is still singular. A word that only looks like a unit stays the word. The heading is what you should hear.

A slash between units is per. An abbreviated unit is the full word.

the car does 100 km/h and the pace is 1 km/h, speed is km/h which is fine i guess.

the limit is 60 miles/hour, rent is $10/lb, the disk moves 100 TB/h and the pace is 1 TB/h.

the link is 10 MB/s, the line is 10 Mb/s, the gap is 3 mm/s.

the API serves 50 requests/sec and light moves at 299792458 m/s, heat capacity is J/K.

Square and cubic stay square and cubic. A signed power is to the power of.

the drop is 1 m/s^2, the sample is 2 g/cm^3, the load is N/m^2, the rate is J/s^-1.

the flow is m^2/s, the block is kg/m^3, the note is m/s2 so leave that one written.

the trickle is 1 m^2/s, the volume is m^3/s, the cube is 1 m^3/s.

the area is ft^2/s and the drop is 1 in^3/s.

the stretch is m^4/s, then 1 m^4/s, then 10 m^4/s. the fall is m/s^4, the curve is m^5/s, the field is kg/m^-23.

A prefix plus a base is the full unit. K alone is kelvin. T alone is tesla. T before a base is tera.

the sample cools at 10 K/s, the file moves at 10 KB/s, the field changes at 2 T/s.

the span grows at 2 Tm/s and the coil changes at 1 mH/s.

the force is N/m, the lamp is cd/m^2, the gas is mol/m^3, the wire is A/m.

the rise is 10 kPa/s and the sun is 2 kW/m^2.

A prefix on a unit that already has one is two words. A micro sign is spoken.

the dose is 5 mg/kg, the mix is µkg/s, the symbol is µkg, the trace is 5 µg, the speck is µg.

hecto, deka, and deci are those prefixes. A bare dam is not a unit.

the plot is 1 hm/s, the lot is 1 dam/s, the step is 1 dm/s.

the wall is the hoover dam. the count is 1 dam. i am here.

Miles, feet, inches, and the everyday rates.

the car does 60 mi/h and 60 mi/hr, the pace is 1 mi/h, pay is $15/hr, spice is $4/oz.

the fall is 32 ft/s, the crawl is 1 ft/s, there are 12 in/ft, the plan is 5 d/wk.

the car does 15 km/L and the trip is 100 km/hr. the car does 60 mph.

One of a unit is singular. Any other quantity is plural. Foot becomes feet. A number glued to the unit is the same reading.

the path is 1 km and the path is 5 km. the board is 1 ft and the board is 5 ft.

the trail is 10km and the road is 12 km long, no space or a space, same unit.

the lamp is 1 kW, the plant is 5 kW, the pack is 1kw, the pill is 5 mg.

the unit is kW, the length is km, the mass is mg. no number, so singular.

dB is decibel. A degree mark is degrees.

the level is 1 dB, the roar is 5 dB, the unit is dB.

the room is 72°F, water freezes at 32°F, water freezes at 0°C, the fever is 98.6°F.

A word that only looks like a unit stays the word. MS with no number is the state, not a unit.

the ham is salty. the state is MS.
