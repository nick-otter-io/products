Heteronyms

Read tomorrow rhymes with reed. Read yesterday, or have read, rhymes with red.

She will read it tomorrow.

She read it yesterday.

I have read this book.

The note was read aloud.

Loud bass, with no other cue, is the instrument, rhyming with base. The fish is the short a.

The bass was loud.

She plays the bass.

He caught a bass in the lake.

Lead the metal rhymes with red. Lead the verb rhymes with reed.

The pipe is made of lead.

She will lead the crew.

A tear on the cheek rhymes with deer. Tear the page rhymes with air.

A tear ran down his cheek.

Tear the page out.

The wind in the trees is the short i. Wind the clock rhymes with find.

The wind was cold.

Wind the clock before bed.

A wound on the skin rhymes with food. Wound the clock rhymes with sound.

The wound was deep.

She wound the clock.

A bow on a box rhymes with go. A bow of the head, or the bow of a ship, rhymes with cow.

Tie a bow on the box.

Take a bow.

The bow of the ship is high.

A row of seats, or row the boat, rhymes with go. A row that is a quarrel rhymes with cow.

Sit in the third row.

Row the boat to shore.

They had a row in the kitchen.

Close the door ends in a z. Stand close ends in an s.

Close the door.

Stand close to the wall.

A live wire rhymes with dive. Live in a city rhymes with give.

A live wire is dangerous.

They live in Ohio.

Nine lives rhymes with dives. She lives rhymes with gives.

A cat has nine lives.

She lives on Oak Street.

The use of a tool ends in an s. Use a tool ends in a z.

The use of a pen is fine.

Use a pen.

The house ends in an s. House a guest ends in a z.

The house is white.

The barn can house four horses.

The desert is the hot place, stress on the first syllable. Desert the post rhymes with dessert.

The desert is dry at noon.

Do not desert the post.

The content of a page stresses the first syllable. Content with a result stresses the second.

The content of the page is short.

She was content with the result.

A minute of time rhymes with limit. A minute crack rhymes with my newt.

Wait one minute.

A minute crack ran through the glass.

Polish the boots rhymes with tallish. He is Polish rhymes with coal-ish.

Polish the boots.

He is Polish.

A mobile clinic rhymes with noble. The city Mobile rhymes with feel.

The clinic is mobile.

She was born in Mobile.

August the month stresses the first syllable. An august silence stresses the second.

August is the eighth month.

An august silence filled the hall.

Reading a letter rhymes with feeding. The city Reading rhymes with bedding.

She is reading the letter.

She grew up in Reading.

A resume for a job is rez-oo-may. Resume the story rhymes with zoom.

Send a resume with the note.

Resume the story after lunch.

A dove on the roof rhymes with love. Dove into the pool rhymes with stove.

A dove sat on the roof.

He dove into the pool.

A sow in a pen rhymes with cow. Sow the seed rhymes with go.

The sow is in the pen.

Sow the seed in May.

The buffet of food is buh-fay. Buffet the pier rhymes with stuff it.

The buffet opens at noon.

The waves buffet the pier.

Sake the drink is sah-kay. For his sake rhymes with take.

The sake was served cold.

She stayed for his sake.

Bologna the meat is buh-lo-nee. Bologna the city is buh-lone-ya.

Slice the bologna thin.

The train stops in Bologna.

Nestle the brand is ness-lee. Nestle the verb is ness-uhl.

Nestle ships the cocoa.

The bird will nestle in the hedge.

The Celtic team is sel-tik. The Celtic language is kel-tik.

The Celtic team won.

The Celtic languages survive.

A gyro sandwich is yee-ro. A gyro in a machine is jy-ro.

The gyro comes with sauce.

The gyro holds the heading.

A pasty face rhymes with tasty. A pasty you eat rhymes with nasty.

His face looked pasty.

She ate a pasty.

Mate the drink is mah-tay. The mate of a ship rhymes with late.

She drank mate after lunch.

The mate of the ship called.

Ravel the composer is ruh-vel. Ravel the thread rhymes with gavel.

Ravel wrote the ballet.

Do not ravel the thread.

Rodeo Drive is ro-day-o. The rodeo event is ro-dee-o.

We walked down Rodeo Drive.

The rodeo starts at noon.

A recount of ballots stresses the first syllable. Recount the story stresses the second.

The recount took a day.

Recount the story from the start.

Axes of a chart rhyme with see. Axes in a shed rhyme with taxes.

The axes of the chart meet at zero.

The axes hang in the shed.

The aged scholar has two syllables. Children aged 10 has one.

The aged scholar spoke.

Children aged 10 may enter.

Jesus the figure is jee-zus. Jesus the Spanish name is hay-soos.

Jesus spoke in parables.

Jesus Garcia signed the form.

Job the figure rhymes with robe. The job of work rhymes with sob.

Job refused to curse the day.

The job starts on Monday.

Refuse the noun rhymes with lettuce. Refuse the verb rhymes with confuse.

Take the refuse to the bin.

Refuse the extra bag.

Separate the adjective ends in it. Separate the verb ends in ate.

The rooms are separate.

Separate the yolks.

Estimate the cost.

A graduate ends in it. Graduate in May ends in ate.

The advocate stood.

Advocate for the change.

The entrance of a building stresses the first syllable. Entrance the crowd stresses the second, and the vowel shifts.

The entrance is shut.

The music will entrance them.

Invalid the adjective stresses the second syllable. An invalid who needs care stresses the first.

The pass is invalid.

An invalid needs the ramp.

The noun or the adjective takes the first-syllable stress. The verb takes the second.

The record is scratched. Record the song again.

The object is a small key. I object to the fee.

The present is on the table. Present the award at noon.

The produce is fresh. The valley will produce wine.

The project ends in May. Project your voice.

The contract is signed. Cold can contract the metal.

The permit is on the desk. Permit one question.

The copy is perfect. Perfect the recipe.

The attribute is optional. Attribute the quote.

The console is on. Console the child.

The conduct was poor. Conduct the interview.

The conflict is old. The dates conflict.

The convert told the story. Convert the file.

The insult stung. Do not insult the host.

The protest was loud. Protest the fine.

The rebel hid. They rebel at dawn.

The suspect waited. I suspect a leak.

Two copies in one sentence keep two readings.

She plays bass, and he caught a bass.

She will read tomorrow what she read yesterday.

The lead pipe will lead the list of repairs.
