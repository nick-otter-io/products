An abbreviation period stays in the sentence, with no pause. A lowercase title is still the title.

ok so dr. smith stayed and main st. market opens at nine, dont wait around.

The sentence ends on the abbreviation.

i live on main st.

Three dots are an ellipsis.

wait at st... then text me.

A sentence-initial A before a word is the article. The capital on A stays, because that is the article at the start of a sentence.

A decimal point stays with the number, which is the whole problem.

done. A fresh copy is on the desk i think.

he said, "A new plan starts monday." and then he left.

A quote between A and the next word stays the article. A lowercase a before a word is the article.

A "quiet" room helps, she called it a real fix.

A dash after a capital A is the letter name.

ok listen, A - you need to be disciplined or this falls apart.

A period after A is the letter name. A before is, are, was, or were is the letter name. A mid-sentence A is the letter name.

here we go. A. First item. then A is for apple, and plan A failed.

One sentence can hold the article and the letter name.

a cat sat in row A and then it left.

Plain, dotted, and spaced spellings are one abbreviation. Lowercase does not split them.

the us signed the treaty, the u.s. signed the treaty, the u. s. signed the treaty. same treaty.

she earned a phd. she earned a ph.d. meet me at 9 am or 9 a.m., i dont mind.

PhD is the letter names.

she earned a phd in art and the phd program is full, which is unfortunate.

A plural or a possessive stays on the abbreviation.

the cpus overheated and the FBI's report is public, its also long.

US and USA stay different.

the us and the usa signed different copies, dont mix them up.

n/a and N/A are the letter names en ay. NA with no slash is the letter names.

the field is n/a. the field is N/A. the field is NA. pick one.

w/ is with. w/o is without. c/o is care of.

tea w/ lemon, tea w/o water, send it c/o jane. thats the whole order.

I/O and A/D are letter names.

i/o is bidirectional and a/d conversion is the next chapter, apparently.

A switch between a letter and a digit splits the token.

b2b sales slowed and the poster is 3d, both of those lines are bad.

An ordinary word stays a word.

install the app, the beam is a laser, nothing fancy.

A title or a Latin abbreviation is the full words.

A company suffix is the full words. A street suffix is the full words.
